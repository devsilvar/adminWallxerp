
PROJECT:  PayMyTax By WallX – In Partnership with FIRS

Product Document
SME Tax Calculation, Reporting & Remittance Tool
Product Name
PayMyTax (PMT) BY WallX
Purpose
The SME Tax Calculation and Remittance Tool enables small businesses (SMEs/MSMEs) to easily calculate their monthly tax obligations, generate tax statements, and remit taxes directly to the Federal Inland Revenue Service (FIRS).
The tool simplifies tax compliance by automatically computing tax payable using the formula:
Tax Payable = 7.5% of Gross Profit
Where:
Gross Profit = Total Monthly Sales − Total Monthly Expenses

The system will automatically compute tax using the formula below.

Gross Profit = Total Monthly Sales − Total Monthly Expenses

If Gross Profit ≤ 0
Tax Payable = 0

3. Tax Calculation Logic (Backend)
The backend should run monthly:
total_sales = SUM(sales_transactions.amount)

total_expenses = SUM(expenses.amount)

gross_profit = total_sales - total_expenses

tax_payable = gross_profit * 0.075
If:
gross_profit <= 0
Then:
tax_payable = 0
